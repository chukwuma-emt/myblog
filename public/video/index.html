    Everything is made on your device: your script, photos and sound stay in this page until you save the video. Encoding uses Mediabunny (MPL-2.0) and, where the browser has no AAC encoder of its own, FFmpeg’s AAC encoder (LGPL-2.1). AI voices use Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and eSpeak NG pronunciation (GPL-3.0). These load only when you use them.
