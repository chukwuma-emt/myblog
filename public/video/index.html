Getting ready…

A blank line starts a new scene. A line that starts with # becomes a title card. Anything in [square brackets] is left out of the video, except a line with just [clip], which makes a scene with no words for a clip on its own.

This is a sample script. Replace it with yours.

Scenes

    Photos and clips fill the scenes in order and repeat if you add fewer than the number of scenes. Clips play in full: the scene stretches to fit and the voiceover waits. Tap Whole clip on a scene to cut that clip to fit instead. Tap a scene to preview it.

    Everything is made on your phone. Your script, clips, photos and sound are kept in this browser and never uploaded anywhere. Encoding uses Mediabunny (MPL-2.0) and, where the browser has no AAC encoder of its own, FFmpeg’s AAC encoder (LGPL-2.1). AI voices use Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and eSpeak NG pronunciation (GPL-3.0). These load only when you use them.
